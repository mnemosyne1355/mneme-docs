How winning works

The draw picks a winner in proportion to weight. Your weight has three parts:

Entries

Each offering is 1 entry = 1 base weight. Ten entries, ten weight. Simple.

Relics

Each relic adds a flat bonus every week it enters — from +5 for a Rare up to +500 for a 1-of-1. All relic bonuses combined are capped at 10% of paid weight: they boost you, they never buy the draw. Full tiers on the Mint tab.

Streaks

Consecutive paid weeks add +10% to your paid weight per week, up to 2×. Skip a week and the streak resets. Loyalty compounds.

A worked example

You hold 10 entries and one Rare relic, on a 4-week streak. Paid weight: 10 × 1.4 = 14. Relic bonus: +5. Your total: 19. If the week's combined weight is 1,900, your chance is 19 ÷ 1,900 — about 1%. Every number is on-chain; check our math.

Relics in the draw

Relics auto-enter every week — rest one before the Sunday snapshot to sit it out. If your relic wins, it's REDEEMED: retired forever, on-chain, visible to everyone. A redeemed relic never enters again.

Free entry

One free entry per wallet, every week. No purchase needed — nobody sits out for being broke. Free entries carry base weight only: no relic or streak bonuses, and all free weight combined is capped at 5% of paid weight.

The mechanics, in detail

Everything below is enforced by the contracts — not by promise. If this page ever disagrees with the code on-chain, the code wins.

Offerings

Entries are called offerings. You buy them in MNEME at a floating price that tracks about $1 — a keeper can re-peg it up to ±25% per update, at most once a day, and it can never fall below 10% of the deploy peg. One offering, one entry for the week.

The Oracle

The draw ceremony. Every Sunday at 20:00 UTC the week's entries lock and the draw settles on a drand randomness beacon — published, timestamped, and verified on-chain. Anyone can post the beacon and settle the draw; nobody needs our permission. Winners are pull-claims: the contract holds the pot until you claim it.

Where the money goes

Every offering payment goes to the prize reserve. When relics mint, the proceeds split on-chain, automatically: 70% seeds MNEME/musebook liquidity (LP tokens burned forever), 20% buys and burns MNEME, 10% funds studio operations. No hands touch it in between.

Claiming

Winners have 7 days to claim on-chain. Unclaimed prizes roll into the next week's pot — they are never kept by the studio.

Fair by construction

Project and team wallets are excluded from every draw, enforced in code. A week with zero paid entries means no draw — the pot simply rolls over.

Relic lifecycle

Relics auto-enter every week. Rest one before the Sunday snapshot to sit it out. If your relic wins, it's REDEEMED — retired permanently, on-chain, visible to everyone. A redeemed relic never enters again.

No guarantees. Draws only run when the rules say they run. Odds depend on total entries and weights in a given week. Unclaimed prizes roll over; they are not owed to anyone until claimed on-chain within the claim window. Past draws say nothing about future ones.

Self-audited, not third-party audited. The contracts went through adversarial self-audit by the studio, documented across multiple review rounds. No independent third-party auditor has reviewed them. Smart contracts can have bugs; funds interacted with them are at your own risk.

The code is canonical. If anything on this page ever disagrees with the deployed contracts, the contracts win. Mechanics described here — entries, weights, exclusions, rollovers, claim windows — are enforced on-chain, not by promise.

Fair play. Project and team wallets are excluded from every draw. Anything the contracts don't permit can't happen — the rules are code, not policy.
