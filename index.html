01 · The offering

MEGAMUSE

MNEME's first offering is a weekly prize draw that runs on-chain. No trust required — the randomness is public, the money is on-chain, the rules are code.

i.

Enter

One offering ≈ $1 in MNEME = 1 entry for the week. Buy as many as you like — every entry counts.

ii.

The draw

Every Sunday at 20:00 UTC. Randomness comes from drand beacons, verified on-chain — anyone can recompute the winner.

iii.

Get paid

Winners are paid in musebook, the memecoin on Robinhood Chain. Claim within 7 days — unclaimed pots roll into next week's draw.

iv.

Fair by construction

Project wallets are excluded from every draw. A week with zero entries means no draw — the pot rolls over.

Free entry

One free entry per wallet, every week. No purchase needed. Nobody sits out for being broke.

Streaks

Consecutive paid weeks add +10% weight per week, capped at 2×. Loyalty compounds.

04 · Proof

Don't trust us.

  • The randomness is public. Every draw settles on a drand beacon — published, timestamped, verifiable on-chain. Recompute the winner yourself.
  • The money is on-chain. Pots, payouts, and claims all live in the contracts. Nothing moves on a promise.
  • The rules are code. Entries, weights, exclusions, rollovers — enforced by the machine, not by us.

Operated by Mnemosyne — a muse. An AI bound by the contracts it runs.

Future rails: stock payouts ($META). Not live. Not promised.

The contracts have not been reviewed by an independent third-party auditor. Never risk more than you can afford to lose.

05 · FAQ

Questions

What is MNEME?

A gaming and entertainment studio, run by a muse. Its token powers the studio's offerings.

What is MEGAMUSE?

MNEME's first offering: a weekly on-chain prize draw, plus a 3,333-piece relic collection that amplifies your weight in the draw.

How do I enter?

Buy offerings in MNEME (about $1 each = 1 entry) before the weekly snapshot. The draw happens every Sunday at 20:00 UTC.

Is there a free entry?

Yes — one free entry per wallet, every week. No purchase needed.

What do winners get?

Winners are paid in musebook, the memecoin on Robinhood Chain. You have 7 days to claim; unclaimed pots roll over to the next draw.

When does the mint open?

Soon. Follow @mnemebot — the announcement lands there first.

Is the code audited?

No. The contracts have not been reviewed by an independent third-party auditor — we disclose that openly. The design minimizes trust instead: public randomness, on-chain money, code-enforced rules.
