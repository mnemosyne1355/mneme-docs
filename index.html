The offering

MEGAMUSE

MNEME's first offering is a weekly prize draw that runs on-chain. No trust required — the randomness is public, the money is on-chain, the rules are code.

i.

Enter

One offering ≈ $1 in MNEME = 1 entry for the week. Buy as many as you like — every entry counts.

ii.

The draw

Every Sunday at 20:00 UTC, settled on a public randomness beacon — anyone can recompute the winner.

iii.

Get paid

Winners claim on-chain within 7 days — unclaimed pots roll into next week's draw. Payout rails live on the Trade tab.

iv.

Fair by construction

Project wallets are excluded from every draw. A week with zero entries means no draw — the pot rolls over.

Free entry

One free entry per wallet, every week. No purchase needed. Nobody sits out for being broke.

Streaks

Consecutive paid weeks add +10% weight per week, capped at 2×. Loyalty compounds.

Fine print

The legal bit.

Eligibility. You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) to participate. MEGAMUSE is void where prohibited by law. It is your responsibility to determine whether participating is legal where you live — if it isn't, sit this one out.

No purchase necessary. One free entry per wallet, every week, no purchase needed. Buying offerings or relics does not improve your odds beyond the entry weight described — a free entry is a real entry.

No guarantees. Draws only run when the rules say they run. Odds depend on total entries and weights in a given week. Unclaimed prizes roll over; they are not owed to anyone until claimed on-chain within the claim window. Past draws say nothing about future ones.

Prizes are volatile crypto, not cash. Winners are paid in musebook, a memecoin on Robinhood Chain. Its value can fall to zero. Nothing here is a promise of profit, yield, or returns — it's a prize draw, not an investment.

Not financial advice. Nothing on this site is financial advice, an offer to sell securities, or a solicitation to buy anything. Digital assets are high-risk. Never risk more than you can afford to lose.

Self-audited, not third-party audited. The contracts went through adversarial self-audit by the studio, documented across multiple review rounds. No independent third-party auditor has reviewed them. Smart contracts can have bugs; funds interacted with them are at your own risk.

The code is canonical. If anything on this page ever disagrees with the deployed contracts, the contracts win. Mechanics described here — entries, weights, exclusions, rollovers, claim windows — are enforced on-chain, not by promise.

Fair play. Project and team wallets are excluded from every draw. Anything the contracts don't permit can't happen — the rules are code, not policy.
